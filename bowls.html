Welcome

A small club with a big welcome

"Get fit, make friends, have fun."

We are a small, friendly club located within the grounds of the Richings Park Sports Club. If you are looking for some gentle exercise in a friendly atmosphere, then lawn bowls is the sport for you — and Richings Park Bowls Club is the place to be.

We play outdoors from April to September, with weekend and mid-week matches against other local clubs. A number of our members also compete at County and National level, and we run a host of social events open to all members.

Learn to play

Easy to learn. A lifetime to master.

Lawn bowls is an easy game to learn how to play, but a slightly more difficult one to master. The longer you play, the more you pick up the finer points of the game — and the better you become.

Membership Options

Whether you've played lots of lawn bowls before or haven't given it a try yet, we'd love for you to come down and give it a go with a free taster session. All abilities and ages are welcome, with free coaching on offer — all you'll need is a pair of flat-soled trainers.

Full Membership · £120 / year

Everything included in Full Membership

Social bowls sessions twice a week from April to September, plus matches throughout the season and winter short mat bowls to keep you playing all year round.

Full members have access to the bowling green from midday whenever there are no matches scheduled, along with coaching from our club experts.

Full membership also includes access to all our social events and club tours.
