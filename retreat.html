The Retreat

Heat, cold and stillness.

The Retreat is our dedicated wellness suite — a full thermal circuit with sauna, steam room, plunge room and ice bath, wrapped around a relax room built for doing nothing at all.

  • Sauna, steam room and plunge room
  • Dedicated ice bath for contrast therapy
  • Red light therapy in a private chamber
  • Relax room with infrared, ambient features and a mini bar
  • Full changing rooms and showers

Two ways in

Members within. Guests without.

The Retreat has two entrances. An internal entrance connects it to the rest of the club, giving members full access as part of their visit. A separate external entrance lets guests book The Retreat on its own, without stepping through the wider club.

  • Internal entrance — full facility access for club members
  • External entrance — standalone bookings for visiting guests
